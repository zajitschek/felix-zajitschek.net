Scientist, Global Citizen, Formby Local

I am an evolutionary biologist (PhD) with an international career spanning research institutions in Australia (UNSW Sydney & Monash), France (CNRS), Sweden, and the USA. My work focuses on understanding complex systems through evidence and long-term data.

My family and I have settled in Formby because of its unique cohesive village structure, its closeness to Liverpool, and its incredible geography. I believe in a future for Formby that manages its success while protecting its character.

Vision for Formby (2025)

Evidence-based solutions for our community's unique challenges.

1. Reclaiming our Streets

I advocate for a Formby-wide "Safe Streets" audit. It’s about making it safer for children to walk to school and for commuters to cycle to our stations. We must reduce "summer gridlock" by prioritizing pedestrian and cycling links for local residents first.

2. Protecting Our "Village Flair"

Formby is a victim of its own beauty. I oppose large-scale "tourist hubs" that invite more traffic. My priority is working with the National Trust to fix waste collection and parking issues, keeping visitor cars off our residential side-streets.

3. Accountability: Facts First

Decisions in Sefton Council should be based on what works, not ideology. Whether it’s scrutinizing Green Belt developments or ensuring youth services are properly supported, I will ensure local solutions preserve Formby’s character.
